# FoodCare - Smart Food Management App 🍲📱

A smart Android application designed for managing food inventory, tracking expiration dates, and helping users reduce food waste.

## 📖 About the Project
**FoodCare** is a mobile application prototype built to help users efficiently manage their food stock. By keeping track of expiration dates and providing an intuitive dashboard interface, FoodCare aims to minimize food waste at the household level and promote better food management habits.

## ✨ Key Features
* **📦 Food Inventory Management**: Easily add, edit, and keep track of all food items currently in your storage.
* **⏳ Expiration Date Tracking**: Monitor when food items are about to expire to consume them in time.
* **📊 Dashboard Interface**: An intuitive and user-friendly home dashboard for a quick overview of your food stock status.
* **🗄️ Database Integration**: Robust local database structure to safely store and retrieve your inventory data.

## 🛠️ Built With
* **Language:** [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
* **UI/Layout:** XML
* **IDE:** [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio)
